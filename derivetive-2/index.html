The Moving Paper: Speed is Slope

A dot moves up and down in a fixed window while a sheet of paper slides steadily past. The dot's motion and the graph on the paper are the same story told two ways, and the speed of the dot is the steepness of the graph.

Inspired by W.W. Sawyer's "What Is Calculus About?" and his moving paper visualization
